# Cemí original-artist animation style

The author hand-drew the source portraits. Their distinctive original geometric and asymmetric facial construction is **canon**, not a generic anime template. Cleaned ink lines, calmer textures, cel-shaped shadows and modest anime eyelid/pose polish are refinements, never a replacement.

**Color Option B:** midnight #1B2634, dark steel #35465B, blue gray #6C8597, warm amber #E7783D, brown #715043 and ink #211C1C. Honor each character's real skin tone under cinematic lighting.

**No writing everywhere.** No random graffiti slogans, floating descriptions, stylized poster prose or text on set walls. Use clean backgrounds and props; add story-essential lettering in post where exact spelling can be controlled.

The author's character-bible continuity overrides early AI explorations. In particular the skinny afro-hair Manny art is not the described Manny: 25, 6'3", massive muscular build, close-cropped hair (adaptation choice), conga player. JC is a different, lean 25-year-old with short dark curls and headphones.

Train the *style* using individually cropped, finished, author-approved portraits **of multiple different characters**, environments and compositions. Do not feed an entire typography-heavy mood board or only one face into the training set. Reserve held-out examples, compare training checkpoints, and keep unapproved art out.

Quality gates: author style recognizability, distinct character identity at multiple angles, consistent color grade, no accidental text, correct anatomy/props, no temporal flicker, human approval.
